I

About

The person behind the mark

Amethystium is the personal mark of Hari Tswei (Pengjia Cui), a computational social scientist trained in artificial intelligence.

My work sits where network science, communication research, and the study of science meet. I map citation and social networks, trace how platforms frame public discourse and scientific recognition, and audit the measurement choices behind claims made from digital traces.

I care about reproducible pipelines, visible provenance, and the difference between a pattern and an explanation. My training includes graduate studies in Computational Social Science at UC San Diego and a B.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence from the Communication University of China. I have worked with research teams at City University of Hong Kong and CUHK-Shenzhen, and in research administration at the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

“Data matters because people matter.”

II

Research

Four lines of work, one question

My research is organized around four lines: computational social science, network analysis, computational communication, and social and information networks. Across them, I ask how fields are organized, how platforms shape public life, and how measurement changes what we can claim — questions that began with recommender systems and still shape how I think about structure and attention.
